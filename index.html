This application is restricted to research and educational use.
Clinical use is prohibited.

  • It must not be used to determine drug dosing, to diagnose, or to guide treatment for any patient
  • It is not a medical device and has received no regulatory approval or clearance
  • Displayed concentrations are theoretical values from population models, not measured concentrations in an individual patient
  • The analgesia readouts are reference indicators from published population statistics and do not establish that any individual patient is adequately analgesed
  • The three implemented PK models give Ce values that differ by up to 1.8-fold for the same dose. Do not treat any single value as the correct answer
  • All clinical decisions must be made by qualified clinicians independently of this application

Assumptions and limitations of the estimate

Patient

22.0

Of the three implemented PK models, only Bae carries a body-weight covariate; Scott-Stanski and Shafer use fixed parameters with no weight covariate. Age, sex, height and ASA-PS are recorded but are not used in the concentration calculation. Scott & Stanski did show that dose requirement falls with age (a 50% reduction from 20 to 89 years), but that is a pharmacodynamic change in brain sensitivity rather than a change in PK, so age does not enter the Ce prediction.
